
# True-Source Dependency Assessment (TSDA)

The **True-Source Dependency Assessment (TSDA)** is a quantitative research method for evaluating whether diversification in the countries directly supplying critical minerals to the United States corresponds to a genuine reduction in underlying dependence on concentrated foreign production and processing sources.

The initial empirical case studies are **gallium and germanium**.

**Researcher:** Blessing Ameh  
**Methodology Date:** September 30, 2026

## Research Question

Did diversification in U.S. gallium and germanium suppliers reduce underlying foreign dependence across production and processing stages, or primarily change the countries recorded as direct suppliers?

## Repository Purpose

This repository documents the development and empirical validation of TSDA. It will contain:

- methodology and data specifications;
- data dictionaries and source inventories;
- raw and processed research data where redistribution is permitted;
- analytical code;
- reproducible tables and figures; and
- empirical results for gallium and germanium.

The methodology distinguishes direct U.S. suppliers from upstream production and processing sources and traces intermediary supplier relationships to estimate the underlying source of U.S. mineral dependence.

## Initial Study Period

- **Pre-shock baseline:** 2019–2022
- **Post-shock period:** 2023–2025
